Student Frequently Asked Questions
Go to My Complaints in the sidebar. Every ticket displays its live state (Submitted, Under Review, In Progress, Resolved). Click "Track Progress" or "View & Track" to inspect the timestamped status history, assigned technician details, and departmental work orders. You can also ask our AI assistant above at any time.
Navigate to My Complaints. Next to your application, click "Edit Application" to update details like issue location, urgency, or description. If the grievance is already resolved or no longer applicable, click "Delete" to remove it. You can also edit or withdraw directly from the Complaint Details page.
Open Profile & Settings from the sidebar. Scroll to the Change Password card, type your current password followed by your new password (minimum 8 characters), and click "Update Password". The update applies immediately for all future sign-ins.
Click "Create Complaint" in the sidebar, select the Voice Input tab, choose your preferred language (English or Hindi), and tap the microphone. Speak naturally about the problem. ProofVoice transcribes your voice in real time with Gnani AI Prisma STT and generates a formal, respectful application addressed to your campus authority.
