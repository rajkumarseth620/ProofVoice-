Grievance Officer Guidelines
Open All Complaints, click on any ticket, and use the Status & Assignment panel. Select the designated department (e.g., IT Services, Estate Office, Sanitation) and optionally name a specific technician. When saved, the student receives a real-time notification with the assignee's name.
Urgent issues (e.g. electrical hazards, lab outages) have a 24-hour target SLA. Standard grievances must be acknowledged within 48 hours and resolved within 5 academic working days. Public remarks added to status changes are visible on the student's live tracking timeline.
