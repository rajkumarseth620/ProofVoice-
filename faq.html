Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ProofVoice voice reporting, formal application drafting, and administrative grievance redressal.

Navigate to the Report an Issue page and ensure the "Voice Input" tab is active. Click the microphone button and grant your browser microphone permission when prompted. Describe your concern naturally. When finished speaking, tap the button again or click "Stop Recording". ProofVoice will convert your speech to text and let you edit the transcript before generating your formal application.
Yes, absolutely. You have complete control. The transcription box is an editable text area where you can fix any misheard words, add room numbers, or append more details before clicking "Generate Formal Application".
ProofVoice strictly requires explicit student confirmation. Before any complaint is formally transmitted to institutional authorities, you are presented with the full structured grievance letter. You can read it, listen to it using the "Read Aloud" button, or edit it. The system will never submit a complaint on your behalf without your explicit checkbox confirmation.
  • Submitted: Your formal application has been logged and assigned an authoritative tracking ID.
  • Under Review: Institutional administrators have received the grievance and are inspecting the details.
  • In Progress: A specific department (e.g., IT Services, Sanitation) or technician has been assigned and is actively working on the fix.
  • Awaiting Student: The administration has requested clarifying information or confirmation from you.
  • Resolved: Work has been completed and verified by the department.
  • Rejected: The issue could not be processed, with a mandatory recorded reason provided.
  • Reopened: The issue recurred or resolution was incomplete according to policy.
ProofVoice features a multi-tenant backend architecture. When you register with your university, college, or school, your account is bound to your specific institutional workspace. Administrator accounts can only view and manage complaints from their own campus. Complaint records are isolated through server-side authorization.
By default, applications are addressed to "The Dean". However, your institution administrator can configure department- and category-specific recipients (such as Chief Hostel Warden for housing issues, Network Administrator for IT, or the Principal). Students can also review and choose the appropriate recipient before confirming submission.
On the Complaint Details and Review pages, click the Read Aloud button. This speaks the formal application or administrative remarks aloud, ensuring accessibility for all campus members.
ProofVoice only collects necessary details such as your student identifier and campus contact email. Audio recordings are processed solely to generate the complaint text and are not retained indefinitely. Private internal administrator notes are strictly hidden from student views.

Need further institutional assistance?

For institutional policy questions, please contact your university's Dean of Student Welfare or Grievance Redressal Committee directly through your campus portal.
