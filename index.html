Problem & Resolution

Reporting an issue should not be complicated.

Traditional complaint methods leave students frustrated and administrative teams overwhelmed with disorganized requests.

Common Campus Challenges
  • ✕ Writing formal, structured grievance applications can be daunting and time-consuming for students.
  • ✕ Students rarely know which specific administrative authority or department to contact.
  • ✕ Paper complaints or scattered emails get lost with zero transparency on current progress.
  • ✕ Communication regarding final resolution or delayed repair timelines remains completely unclear.
How ProofVoice Solves It
  • ✓ Voice-first reporting: Describe your issue naturally by speaking or typing in your words.
  • ✓ Structured application generation: Automated assistance drafts formal institutional letters.
  • ✓ Explicit confirmation: Review and verify every word before authoritative submission.
  • ✓ Transparent timeline tracking: Follow every update from submission to verified resolution.
Simple 4-Step Process

From voice note to verified resolution

A streamlined workflow engineered for students and institutional authorities alike.

1

Speak or Type

2

Comprehensive Platform

Built for modern institutional accountability

Every tool needed to bridge the communication gap between campus members and administrative desks.

1. Voice-Based Reporting

Enables students to speak their concerns directly into their device microphone with real-time waveform feedback.

2. Speech-to-Text

Converts recorded voice inputs accurately into editable text transcripts ready for administrative review.

3. Formal Application Assistance

Automatically structures informal accounts into formal institutional letters complete with salutations and impact summaries.

4. Application Preview and Editing

Review the drafted grievance document and edit any wording or specifics before granting submission authorization.

5. Unique Complaint Tracking ID

Every grievance receives an authoritative sequence identifier for persistent records and easy institutional follow-up.

6. Real-Time Status Updates

Watch your complaint move through transparent milestones: Submitted, Under Review, In Progress, and Resolved.

7. Text-to-Speech for Updates

Listen aloud to formal application drafts and administrative remarks with integrated audio playback.

8. Department Assignment

Directs issues to appropriate campus teams such as IT Services, Water Works, Electrical Maintenance, or Hostel Life.

9. Duplicate Complaint Suggestions

Detects existing open tickets at identical campus locations to prevent duplicated work orders and redundant tasks.

10. Institution-Specific Dashboards

Dedicated workspaces with custom departments, categories, and permission tiers for each registered institution.

11. Analytics and Reports

Track resolution times, volume trends, and recurring maintenance bottlenecks with actionable charts.

12. Notifications and Reminders

Keeps both complainants and technicians informed through timely in-app notification alerts at every status change.

Multi-Tenant Architecture

One platform. Multiple institutions. Independent workspaces.

ProofVoice is engineered to serve universities, polytechnics, and schools independently. Each institution operates in its own isolated administrative workspace without sharing complaint records with one another.

Register Your Institution

Server-Side Authorization & Isolation

Each institution's complaint data is isolated through server-side authorization. Students can access only their own submissions, and institutional administrators can review records only within their verified organization.

Flexible Campus Locations

ProofVoice does not enforce hardcoded buildings or specific campuses. Any facility, wing, floor, or room can be specified flexibly across different institutions worldwide.
